## 1. Brand Essence

**Ember** = the small, persistent glow that becomes a fire. A single backed pledge. A milestone delivered. The thing that catches.

The name does a lot of work. It signals:
- **Warmth** — backing someone's idea is a human act, not a financial transaction
- **Permanence** — embers don't go out; smart contracts don't forget
- **Origin** — every successful project started as someone willing to commit first
- **Restraint** — embers, not infernos. Quiet competence over hype.

The product itself is a non-custodial, milestone-based crowdfunding platform on Morph L2. But the brand isn't "DeFi crowdfunding." It's the answer to *"how do I back something and not get burned?"* — a question with an obvious, intentional pun.

## 2. Positioning

**One-line positioning:**
> Ember is institutional-grade onchain crowdfunding where funds release as creators deliver — not before.

**The hero line:**
> Fund the future, one milestone at a time.

**Three things we are:**
1. **Trust-first.** Cryptographic guarantees, not platform promises.
2. **Algorithmic.** Code decides fund release. No human discretion. No customer-service-as-justice.
3. **Stablecoin-native.** USDC on Morph. No tokens you've never heard of. No price volatility on a pledge.

**Three things we are not:**
1. **A token launch platform.** We do not sell access. We do not run sales. We do not airdrop.
2. **An investment marketplace.** Pledges are utility, not securities. We do not narrate upside.
3. **A Web2 storefront with a wallet skin.** The blockchain is load-bearing, not decorative.

## 3. Naming & Capitalization

- **Product name:** Ember (not "ember," not "EMBER")
- **Never** "Project Ember" in customer-facing copy
- **Lowercase only** in URLs, code identifiers, and CLI commands: `ember.app`, `ember-cli`, `@ember/contracts`
- **Acceptable variants in marketing:** "Ember" (preferred), "Ember Platform" (when disambiguation needed against the word "ember"), never "the Ember"
- **Possessive:** "Ember's escrow contract" ✓ — "The Ember escrow contract" ✓ — "An Ember escrow contract" ✓

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Project Ember" (internal-only, dead now)
- ❌ "EMBR" (we don't have a token)
- ❌ "Ember.fi", "Ember.xyz" branding (just Ember)

**Tagline placements:**
- Hero: "Fund the future, one milestone at a time."
- Short: "Crowdfunding that delivers."
- Boilerplate footer: "Ember. Secured by Morph L2."

## 4. Voice & Tone

Ember writes the way a competent engineer talks to a smart founder: **direct, specific, confident, never patronizing**.

### Voice characteristics

| Attribute | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** | Lead with the point. Cut hedges. | ✓ "Funds release when milestones are verified." ✗ "Generally speaking, our smart contracts may release funds upon successful milestone verification." |
| **Specific** | Numbers and mechanisms over adjectives. | ✓ "85% of backers approved Milestone 1." ✗ "The community overwhelmingly approved." |
| **Confident** | State the position. Don't apologize for it. | ✓ "We don't hold your funds." ✗ "We're proud to say that Ember is non-custodial." |
| **Crypto-fluent** | Use the right terms. Explain on demand. | ✓ "TVL" with a tooltip ✗ Avoiding "TVL" because it might confuse |
| **Warm, not soft** | Human, but never cute. | ✓ "Welcome back. Two milestones need your vote." ✗ "Hey friend! 👋 Got some cool stuff for ya 🔥" |

### Tone by surface

- **Marketing pages:** Aspirational but specific. Lead with proof points.
- **Product UI:** Functional and quiet. Let actions speak. Avoid exclamation marks.
- **Empty states:** Encouraging but practical. Tell users what to do, not how they should feel.
- **Errors:** Honest about what went wrong. Specific about how to fix it. Never blame the user.
- **Legal & disclaimers:** Plain English. We're not hiding anything.
- **Emails:** Short. Subject line says everything. Body confirms it.

### Words we use

- **Pledge** (not "donate" — they get something; not "invest" — they're not buying a security)
- **Backer** (not "investor," not "user," not "customer")
- **Creator** (not "campaigner," not "founder")
- **Campaign** (not "project" when referring to the funding instance; "project" is fine for the underlying thing being built)
- **Milestone** (the unit of trust)
- **Tranche** (in technical contexts only; "fund release" in customer-facing copy)
- **Escrow** (this is what holds funds — say it)
- **Onchain** (one word; not "on-chain")

### Words we don't use

- ❌ **"Investors"** (creates securities-law ambiguity)
- ❌ **"ROI" / "returns" / "yield" / "profit"** (we're not selling expected returns)
- ❌ **"Game-changing," "revolutionary," "disrupting"** (vague and unearned)
- ❌ **"Trustless"** (overloaded; say "trust-protected" or "non-custodial")
- ❌ **"Web3," "DeFi" as descriptors of the product** (technologies, not value propositions)
- ❌ **Emojis in product UI** (sparingly OK in social; never in app)

## 5. Logo

The wordmark is **"Ember"** set in Inter ExtraBold (800), letter-spacing -0.01em. The mark color is the **primary** color (`#b72301`) on light surfaces and **primary-fixed-dim** (`#ffb4a4`) on dark surfaces.

Optionally, a square icon mark sits to the left of the wordmark. The icon is a stylized glowing ember — a soft-edged warm-red shape. Always pair with the wordmark in headers; the icon alone is reserved for favicons, app icons, and tight spaces.

### Clear space

Minimum clear space around the logo lockup equals the height of the lowercase "e" in the wordmark.

### Minimum size

- Wordmark only: 64px wide minimum (web), 16pt minimum (print)
- Icon + wordmark lockup: 96px wide minimum

### Don't

- ❌ Don't recolor the wordmark to anything other than primary, primary-fixed-dim, on-surface (black), or on-primary (white on solid primary background)
- ❌ Don't stretch, skew, or rotate
- ❌ Don't apply drop shadows or glows to the wordmark itself (the icon mark may have a subtle glow as an integral part of the mark)
- ❌ Don't place on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid scrim

## 6. Color System

Ember uses a **Material Design 3-inspired token system** with warm, fire-aligned primaries and a forest-green tertiary for verified/successful states. The system is structured so a dark mode can be added cleanly post-MVP via `data-theme="dark"`.

### Primary — Ember Red

The signature color. Used for primary actions, brand mark, active milestones, and "live" indicators.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `primary` | `#b72301` | Buttons, links, active states, brand mark |
| `on-primary` | `#ffffff` | Text/icons on top of primary backgrounds |
| `primary-container` | `#ff5733` | Decorative warm accents, hero highlights |
| `on-primary-container` | `#580c00` | Text on primary-container |
| `primary-fixed` | `#ffdad3` | Soft primary backgrounds (badges, hover states) |
| `primary-fixed-dim` | `#ffb4a4` | Dark mode primary, icon-marks on dark |
| `on-primary-fixed` | `#3d0600` | Text on primary-fixed |
| `on-primary-fixed-variant` | `#8c1800` | Secondary text on primary-fixed |
| `inverse-primary` | `#ffb4a4` | Primary for inverse surfaces |
| `surface-tint` | `#b72301` | Subtle primary cast on elevated surfaces |

### Secondary — Neutral Gray

Used sparingly for tertiary actions, muted UI, and dividers that need warmth.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `secondary` | `#5f5e5f` | Subdued text, tertiary actions |
| `on-secondary` | `#ffffff` | Text on secondary |
| `secondary-container` | `#e2dfe0` | Tag backgrounds, chip fills |
| `on-secondary-container` | `#636263` | Text on secondary-container |
| `secondary-fixed` | `#e5e2e3` | Fixed neutral surface |
| `secondary-fixed-dim` | `#c8c6c7` | Dimmed neutral surface |

### Tertiary — Verified Green

Used **only** for success, verification, "passed," "completed," "released," "approved." Never for primary actions.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `tertiary` | `#006c4b` | "Completed" status, verified badges |
| `on-tertiary` | `#ffffff` | Text on tertiary |
| `tertiary-container` | `#00a574` | Brighter success backgrounds |
| `on-tertiary-container` | `#003120` | Text on tertiary-container |
| `tertiary-fixed` | `#68fcbf` | Soft success backgrounds |
| `tertiary-fixed-dim` | `#45dfa4` | Dimmed mint accents |

### Error — Destructive Red

Distinct from primary. Used for rejection, refund warnings, validation failures.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `error` | `#ba1a1a` | Error text, destructive buttons, rejected votes |
| `on-error` | `#ffffff` | Text on error |
| `error-container` | `#ffdad6` | Error backgrounds, soft warnings |
| `on-error-container` | `#93000a` | Text on error-container |

### Surfaces — The 9-step Neutral Ramp

The page sits at `background`. Cards layer up from `surface-container-lowest` (white) through five steps to `surface-container-highest`. This gives every layout a clean sense of elevation without shadows.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `background` | `#f8f9fa` | Page background |
| `surface` | `#f8f9fa` | Same as background, used for top app bar |
| `surface-bright` | `#f8f9fa` | Bright accent surface |
| `surface-dim` | `#d9dadb` | Subtle dimmed surface |
| `surface-container-lowest` | `#ffffff` | Card backgrounds, primary content surface |
| `surface-container-low` | `#f3f4f5` | Subtle layered cards |
| `surface-container` | `#edeeef` | Mid-layer surface |
| `surface-container-high` | `#e7e8e9` | Elevated chip/badge backgrounds |
| `surface-container-highest` | `#e1e3e4` | Highest neutral elevation |
| `surface-variant` | `#e1e3e4` | Form field backgrounds, progress bar tracks |
| `inverse-surface` | `#2e3132` | Toast backgrounds, dark callouts |
| `inverse-on-surface` | `#f0f1f2` | Text on inverse-surface |

### Text & Outlines

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `on-background` | `#191c1d` | Primary body text |
| `on-surface` | `#191c1d` | Text on surface and surface-container-* |
| `on-surface-variant` | `#5b403a` | Muted text, captions (warm gray-brown) |
| `outline` | `#8f7069` | Strong dividers, input borders (warm taupe) |
| `outline-variant` | `#e4beb6` | Subtle dividers, card borders (warm pink-gray) |

### Color usage rules

1. **One primary action per view.** Multiple `primary` buttons on a single screen is a UX failure.
2. **Tertiary (green) only for verified/success/completed.** Never for "next" or "continue" — that's primary.
3. **Error red and primary red are different colors.** They look distinct on screen. Don't substitute.
4. **Use the surface ramp instead of shadows** to create depth. Layered surfaces > drop shadows.
5. **Body text is `on-surface` (`#191c1d`).** Captions and metadata are `on-surface-variant` (`#5b403a`).
6. **Borders are `outline-variant` by default** (subtle, warm). Use `outline` only when emphasis is needed (form inputs, segmented controls).

## 7. Typography

Ember uses **Inter** as a single-family system, with weight and size carrying all the hierarchy. No display serif. No mono in marketing copy (mono is reserved for wallet addresses, code, and technical labels).

### Type scale

| Token | Size / Line | Weight | Letter-spacing | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `display-lg` | 48 / 56 | 700 | -0.02em | Hero on desktop |
| `display-lg-mobile` | 36 / 44 | 700 | -0.02em | Hero on mobile |
| `headline-lg` | 32 / 40 | 600 | -0.01em | Page titles |
| `headline-md` | 24 / 32 | 600 | 0 | Section titles, card titles |
| `body-lg` | 18 / 28 | 400 | 0 | Lead paragraphs, hero subhead |
| `body-md` | 16 / 24 | 400 | 0 | Default body |
| `label-md` | 14 / 20 | 500 | 0.01em | Buttons, form labels, nav |
| `label-sm` | 12 / 16 | 600 | 0 | Eyebrow labels, badges, metadata |

### Mono (technical contexts only)

Use **JetBrains Mono** at 13–14px for: wallet addresses, transaction hashes, contract names, code snippets, indexer references. Do **not** use mono in marketing copy or headlines.

### Typography rules

1. **No more than three sizes on a single card.** Hierarchy through size, not color.
2. **Eyebrow labels are uppercase, `label-sm`, `tracking-wider` (≈0.05em).** Used to anchor sections.
3. **Numbers in `display-lg` weight 700** for stats panels. Never use serif numerals.
4. **Wallet addresses always truncated:** `0x74a...8f92`. Full address available on click.
5. **Don't justify text.** Left-align. Center-align only for hero copy and stats blocks.
6. **Line length:** body text ≤ 70 characters per line on desktop, ≤ 45 on mobile.

## 8. Spacing, Layout & Radius

### Spacing tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `base` | 8px | The base unit. All spacing is a multiple. |
| `gutter` | 24px | Grid gutter between cards/columns |
| `margin-mobile` | 16px | Outer page padding on mobile |
| `margin-desktop` | 64px | Outer page padding on desktop |
| `max-width` | 1280px | Maximum content width |

**Rule:** all custom spacing snaps to an 8px grid. 4px is only allowed for icon-text gaps and tight typographic adjustments.

### Layout patterns

- **Containers:** `max-w-[1280px] mx-auto px-4 md:px-16`
- **Grid:** 12-column at desktop, gutter 24px. Most layouts use 7/5 or 8/4 splits for content + sidebar, and 1/3 grids for card rows.
- **Vertical rhythm:** Section padding `py-24` on marketing pages, `py-12` inside the app shell.
- **Sticky header height:** 80px (`h-20`) — keep this constant.

### Border radius

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `DEFAULT` | 2px | Almost never — too small for the brand |
| `lg` | 4px | Small chips, tight badges |
| `xl` | 8px | Buttons, inputs, small cards |
| `full` | 12px | Cards, modals, panels, primary buttons |
| (Tailwind) `rounded-full` | 9999px | Avatars, progress bars, status dots, pills |

**Default radius for product surfaces is `rounded-xl` (8px)** for buttons and inputs, **`rounded-2xl` (16px)** for hero cards and large containers, **`rounded-full`** for pills and avatars.

## 9. Elevation & Shadows

Ember leans on **surface layering** over shadows. Shadows are used sparingly and only for "lifted" interactive elements.

| Level | Treatment | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Flat on background | Page sections |
| 1 | `bg-surface-container-lowest` + `border-outline-variant` | Default cards |
| 2 | Level 1 + `shadow-sm` | Hover state on cards |
| 3 | Level 1 + `shadow-md` | Primary CTAs |
| 4 | Level 1 + `shadow-lg shadow-primary/20` | Hero CTAs, featured cards |
| 5 | Level 1 + `shadow-2xl shadow-primary/10` | Hero illustration card |

**Rule:** never stack shadows on adjacent elements. If two cards sit side by side, both are level 1 — the elevation difference between them is zero.

## 10. Iconography

Ember uses **Material Symbols Outlined** as the sole icon system. Outlined by default; switch to filled (`'FILL' 1`) only for status emphasis (e.g., `shield_locked` in the hero, active milestone flags).

### Rules

- Icon size matches the line-height of adjacent text:
  - Next to `label-sm` → 14–16px
  - Next to `label-md` / `body-md` → 20px
  - Next to `headline-md` → 24px
  - Standalone in feature cards → 24–32px
- Icons inherit text color unless they're carrying their own semantic color (success = tertiary, error = error)
- Use `aria-hidden="true"` on decorative icons; pair with text or `aria-label` for interactive icons
- Don't mix Material Symbols with another icon set (lucide, heroicons, etc.) in the same view

### Canonical icon vocabulary

| Concept | Icon |
|---|---|
| Wallet | `account_balance_wallet` |
| Escrow / locked funds | `account_balance` |
| Backer | `group` |
| Time remaining | `schedule` |
| Settings | `settings` |
| Update / announcement | `campaign` |
| Message / inbox | `mail` |
| Network layer | `layers` |
| Verified / shield | `shield_locked` (filled when emphasized) |
| Active flag / milestone | `flag` (filled when active) |
| Voting | `how_to_vote` |
| Payment | `payments` |
| Submit proof | `cloud_upload` |
| Trending up | `trending_up` |

## 11. Components

### Buttons

| Variant | Style | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | `bg-primary text-on-primary rounded-xl px-6 py-3 font-label-md shadow-sm hover:bg-surface-tint` | One per view. The single highest-priority action. |
| **Primary, hero** | Primary + `px-8 py-4 shadow-lg shadow-primary/20 hover:scale-[0.98]` | Hero CTAs only. |
| **Outline** | `bg-surface text-on-surface border border-outline rounded-xl px-6 py-3 hover:bg-surface-container-low` | Secondary action. Lives next to primary. |
| **Outline, primary-tinted** | `text-primary border border-primary rounded-xl px-6 py-3 hover:bg-primary-fixed` | Tertiary-prominence creator actions (e.g., "Launch Project") |
| **Ghost** | `text-on-surface hover:bg-surface-container-low rounded-xl px-4 py-2` | Inline actions, nav items |
| **Destructive** | `bg-error text-on-error rounded-xl ...` | Cancel campaign, take-down (admin), withdraw confirmations |

**Button rules:**
- All buttons use `rounded-xl` (8px). Pills (`rounded-full`) are reserved for status badges.
- Minimum touch target: 44×44px (height: `py-3` minimum)
- Disabled state: `opacity-50 cursor-not-allowed` — no pointer events
- Loading state: replace text with spinner; keep width stable to prevent layout shift

### Cards

The default card is a **Level 1 surface**:

```html
<div class="bg-surface-container-lowest border border-outline-variant rounded-xl p-6 shadow-sm">
  ...
</div>
```

- **Featured/hero card:** `rounded-2xl shadow-2xl shadow-primary/10`
- **Subtle card (sidebar widget):** `bg-surface-container-lowest border border-outline-variant rounded-xl p-6`
- **Active card (e.g., active milestone):** add `border-2 border-primary-fixed-dim`

### Status badges (pills)

| Status | Style |
|---|---|
| **Completed** | `bg-surface-container-low text-tertiary` |
| **Active / In progress** | `bg-primary-fixed text-on-primary-fixed-variant` |
| **Awaiting / Pending** | `bg-surface-container-high text-on-surface-variant` |
| **Locked / Future** | `bg-surface-container-high text-on-surface-variant` + lock icon |
| **Failed / Rejected** | `bg-error-container text-on-error-container` |

All pills: `px-2 py-1 rounded text-label-sm font-semibold`.

### Status dots

A 6–8px circle preceding a status label. Color matches the semantic state. Use `animate-pulse` only on `primary` dots that indicate live/active action requiring user attention.

### Progress bars

```html
<div class="w-full h-2 bg-surface-variant rounded-full overflow-hidden">
  <div class="h-full bg-primary rounded-full" style="width: 49%"></div>
</div>
```

- Track is always `bg-surface-variant`
- Fill is `bg-primary` (funding progress), `bg-tertiary` (success/completion), or split (for voting results — tertiary YES / error NO)
- Height `h-2`. `h-1` for compact contexts, `h-3` only for hero progress visualizations

### Inputs

```html
<input class="w-full bg-surface-container-lowest border border-outline rounded-xl px-4 py-3 font-body-md text-on-surface focus:border-primary focus:ring-2 focus:ring-primary/20 outline-none" />
```

- Default border `outline`
- Focus: `border-primary` + soft primary ring
- Error: `border-error` + helper text below in `text-error`
- Label is always visible above the input. No placeholder-as-label.

### Timeline (milestone tracker)

The vertical timeline is one of Ember's signature components. Pattern:

- Left rail: 2px line in `border-surface-container-high`
- Status markers: 16–20px circles on the rail, colored by milestone state
- Each card sits to the right of its marker, with `ml-8` and the rail anchored at `ml-4`
- Active milestone gets `border-2 border-primary-fixed-dim` + shadow

## 12. Imagery

Ember does **not** use stock photography of "happy team members at laptops" or generic crypto-bro futurism. Visual support comes from:

1. **Functional UI mockups** — show the product doing its job (e.g., the hero escrow card). The product *is* the imagery.
2. **Abstract gradient surfaces** — warm gradients (primary → primary-container) at low opacity, often combined with `mix-blend-luminosity` over subtle textures for campaign-card backgrounds where no creator-supplied image exists.
3. **Creator-supplied images** — campaign covers and milestone proofs come from creators. We render them edge-to-edge inside the card with `object-cover`, no decorative overlays.

### Image rules

- Avatars: circular, with `border border-outline-variant`
- Campaign covers: 16:9 aspect, `object-cover`, `rounded-t-xl` if at top of card
- All product imagery on dark backgrounds gets a subtle warm cast — never cold blue tint
- Decorative gradient backdrops: keep opacity ≤ 0.5 so foreground text stays AAA-contrast

## 13. Motion

Ember motion is **subtle and purposeful**. We don't bounce. We don't spring. We don't celebrate.

| Motion | Use | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| `transition-colors` | Hover state on links, buttons, nav | 200ms ease |
| `transition-transform` | Card image zoom on hover | 500ms ease |
| `hover:scale-[0.98]` | Primary CTA press feedback | 150ms ease-out |
| `animate-pulse` | Live/awaiting-action indicators only | Default Tailwind |
| `transition-opacity` | Fade in skeleton → loaded content | 300ms ease |

**Don't:**
- Animate page loads with full-page wipes
- Use parallax on marketing pages
- Animate dollar amounts counting up — display the final number immediately

## 15. Accessibility

Ember meets **WCAG 2.1 AA** at minimum. The brand system was designed around contrast.

- **Body text on background:** `#191c1d` on `#f8f9fa` = **~16.5:1** ✓ AAA
- **Primary on white:** `#b72301` on `#ffffff` = **~6.8:1** ✓ AA (and AAA for large text)
- **On-primary on primary:** `#ffffff` on `#b72301` = **~6.8:1** ✓ AA
- **On-surface-variant on background:** `#5b403a` on `#f8f9fa` = **~8.6:1** ✓ AAA

### Rules

- Never use color alone to communicate status — always pair with an icon or text
- All interactive elements have a visible focus ring (`focus:ring-2 focus:ring-primary/40`)
- Buttons and links have minimum 44×44px touch targets
- Form errors are announced to screen readers via `aria-describedby`
- Animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## 16. Do / Don't

### Voice

✓ "Funds release in tranches as milestones are verified."
✗ "Embers's revolutionary milestone tech is changing crowdfunding forever! 🔥🚀"

✓ "You're backing a project — not buying a security."
✗ "Get in early on the next big thing."

### Visual

✓ Single primary button per view; outline button as secondary
✗ Three red buttons side by side

✓ Status pill: `bg-surface-container-low text-tertiary` for "Completed"
✗ Status pill: solid bright green with white text

✓ Layered surfaces using the 9-step ramp
✗ Heavy drop shadows on every card

### Logo

✓ Wordmark + primary color + clear space respected
✗ Wordmark on a busy photo, recolored teal

### Iconography

✓ Material Symbols Outlined throughout
✗ Mixing Material Symbols with Lucide or emoji in the same view

## 17. Tailwind Reference

The full Tailwind config is the source of truth. Here is the canonical theme extension that the mocks ship with — copy this directly into `tailwind.config.ts`:

```ts
import type { Config } from "tailwindcss";

export default {
  darkMode: "class",
  theme: {
    extend: {
      colors: {
        // Primary
        primary: "#b72301",
        "on-primary": "#ffffff",
        "primary-container": "#ff5733",
```

## A note for the coding agent

When implementing UI per SPEC.md and AGENT.md, this document **supersedes** any styling defaults in those files. Where AGENT.md says "use Tailwind v4," this document is what Tailwind is being themed with. Where AGENT.md says "shadcn/ui primitives, customized to brand," **this is the brand** you customize to.

Three operational reminders:
1. **No new colors.** If you can't find a token here, you don't need it.
2. **No new font sizes.** If you can't find a token here, design wants to talk first.
3. **Material Symbols only.** No Lucide, no Heroicons, no emoji icons — even if AGENT.md mentioned them as defaults. The mocks established Material Symbols, so we ship Material Symbols.
